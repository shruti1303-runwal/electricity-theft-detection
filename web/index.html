How it works

1. Clean

Interpolate missing readings, cap sensor spikes at mean + 2σ, scale each customer to 0–1 so the model learns the shape of usage, not the house size.

2. Engineer features

Zero-day share, before/after consumption drop, weekly irregularity, day-to-day jumpiness, flat-line days, long-term trend.

3. Wide & Deep CNN

Wide: dense layers over the full series. Deep: Conv2D over a 52 × 7 weeks-by-days image, since honest homes have a weekly rhythm that theft breaks. Stats: the engineered features.

4. Tune for the business

Class weights for the rare thief class, a hyperparameter search on validation AUC, and a decision threshold chosen to minimise false positives while keeping recall.

Inspired by Zheng et al., Wide and Deep CNNs for Electricity-Theft Detection, IEEE TII 2018. Trained in Python/Keras; weights exported to TensorFlow.js so this page needs no server.
